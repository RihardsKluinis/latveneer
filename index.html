The mill

A new mill in the Jēkabpils industrial park.

Our factory sits in the industrial park of Jēkabpils, Latvia, close to the birch forests we buy from. Logs arrive metres from the peeling lines, and every pallet that leaves can be traced back to its source.

Why LatVeneer

A young mill, built deliberately.

LatVeneer was founded in 2025, when we signed for our facility in the Jēkabpils industrial park in January and brought the production plant in by sea — sixteen 40-foot containers of peeling, drying and sorting equipment. By September 2025 the first finished pallets were loaded, and our FSC® chain-of-custody certificate was issued the same month.

The product is rotary-cut (spindleless-peeled) core birch veneer from Latvian silver birch: two peeling lines at 1300 mm and 1600 mm, a 32-metre four-belt dryer fired with our own recycled woodchips, and a thickness tolerance held within ±0.05 mm. Every pallet is traced individually — we run both FSC® Percentage and Transfer systems and are building EUDR due-diligence (DDS) reporting alongside. In 2026 the Latvian State Revenue Service awarded the company its highest A rating for 2025.

Two further lines are being installed. The story so far — from empty hall to full production — is documented month by month in our news diary, and the full specification is on the product page. Forest owners can find log-buying terms in English and Latvian.
