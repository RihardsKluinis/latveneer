Quality

Requirements for logs.

  • Curvature per metre no more than 2 cm (within the cut)
  • S-shaped curvature is not allowed
  • Minimal branching (no more than three branches per metre)
  • Freshly sawn (staining logs are not suitable for production)
  • Maximum diameter 40 cm (if exceeded, the log will be marked as defective)

Contact

Selling roundwood?

Call or write with your volumes and location and we'll agree the price and delivery time. Deliveries arrive metres from the peeling lines that turn your logs into FSC®-certified birch veneer.
