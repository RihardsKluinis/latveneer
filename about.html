About LatVeneer

A new veneer mill, built with purpose.

Our mill sits in the industrial park of Jēkabpils, minutes from the Latvian birch forests we source from. We work only with silver birch (Betula pendula), and every pallet that leaves the gate can be traced back to the source of its timber — peeling, drying, sorting and palletising all happen under one roof.

Traceable from forest to pallet

Every pallet is traced on its own.

FSC® chain of custody. Licence code FSC-C219509. We operate both the FSC® Percentage and Transfer systems and trace every product pallet individually, so each order can carry the appropriate FSC® claim.

EUDR. We already work with the EUDR system and are building due-diligence (DDS) reporting alongside it.

SRS rating A. The Latvian State Revenue Service's highest rating, awarded for our work in 2025.

Thinking green

A dryer that runs on its own offcuts.

  • Our veneer dryer runs entirely on wood chips made right beside the peeling lines.
  • A water-curtain filtration system cleans the dryer's emissions.
  • Surplus chips go to local power plants as renewable energy.
