Production process

From log to finished veneer.

Logs are peeled on two spindleless peeling lines at natural moisture levels, without presoaking. Veneer thickness and quality are monitored throughout production.

Freshly peeled veneer rests for at least 48 hours, allowing moisture to redistribute naturally.

A 32 m four-belt dryer, powered by recycled woodchips, dries the veneer to the required moisture level.

Hourly inspections ensure the ideal moisture content of 4–8% is maintained.

Each sheet is manually inspected and sorted into grade packs to ensure consistent quality.

Good to know

Veneer. Simply explained.

What is spindleless (rotary) peeling?

The log rotates against a long knife, peeling the wood away in one continuous sheet — like unrolling a roll of paper. A spindleless lathe grips the log with drive rollers instead of centre spindles, allowing it to peel down to a very small core and turn more of each birch log into usable veneer.

Rotary-cut vs sliced veneer — what's the difference?

Rotary-cut veneer is peeled continuously around the log, producing broad grain and full-size sheets — the standard for plywood and moulded-plywood parts. Sliced veneer is cut one sheet at a time from a flitch to create decorative face grain. We produce rotary-cut birch core veneer.

What does FSC®-certified veneer mean?

It means the wood can be traced from responsibly managed forests through every step of production under our FSC® chain of custody. We operate both FSC® Percentage and Transfer systems and trace every pallet individually, providing the traceability needed for FSC®-labelled orders.

Which thicknesses and sheet sizes can you supply?

Our standard is 1.5 mm; 1.0–3.0 mm is available on request, held to a thickness tolerance of ±0.05 mm. Sheets come in six sizes: 1300 × 1300, 1300 × 1600, 1300 × 2600, 1600 × 1300, 1600 × 1600 and 1600 × 3100 mm.

Which grades do you sell?

ABCD and E grade packs. Every sheet is inspected by hand and sorted by the criteria in our grading table — knots, splits, discoloration, rot, dirt and cut-off — so a pack contains what its grade allows and nothing worse.

How do I get a price or a sample?

Prices are quoted on request. Send the sheet size, thickness, grades, volume and delivery country to our sales manager or use the sample form, and we will reply within one working day. Samples are cut from the current production run.

In the industrial park of Jēkabpils, Latvia (Ausekļa iela 3A, LV-5201), about 140 km by road from the port of Riga. The A6 highway links the mill to Riga and the rest of the Baltic road network.
